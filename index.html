  1. Click the Copy signature button above. It will say “Copied”.
  2. Open Gmail settings (the gear at the top right, then See all settings).
  3. Scroll down to Signature. Click Create new, name it Amber, and click Create. (If you already have one, click it and delete everything in the box.)
  4. Click inside the big empty box and paste: Command + V on a Mac, Ctrl + V on Windows.
  5. Just below, under Signature defaults, choose Amber for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  6. Scroll to the very bottom of the page and click Save Changes.
To check it: click Compose. The signature should be there with your photo. Send yourself a test email and tap the phone number, the email address, and the street address. Each one is a link.
On your phone: the Gmail app uses this same signature automatically, as long as “Mobile signature” is empty in the app (Gmail app → menu → Settings → your account → Signature settings).
